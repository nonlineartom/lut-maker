SpyderBro

Log screenshot → Rec.709 .cube LUT, in your browser. Bring your own chart.

1

Load a screenshot

A still pulled from a clip in the camera's native log gamma. PNG keeps the values intact; JPEG is fine for most workflows.

2

Align the chart

Drag the four markers onto the centres of the four corner patches: TL → TR → BR → BL. Inner patches are bilinearly interpolated, so the chart can sit at any angle or perspective.

3

Patches

Sampled vs. reference Rec.709 values. Click any reference swatch to override its value if your specific chart prints with different numbers.
