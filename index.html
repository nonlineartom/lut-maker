SpyderCheckr → Rec.709 LUT Maker

Drop in a screenshot of a Datacolor SpyderCheckr photographed in your camera's log format, line up the chart, and generate a .cube LUT that converts that log signal to Rec.709.

1 Load screenshot

Use a still from a clip in the camera's native log gamma. PNG keeps the values intact; JPEG is fine for most workflows.

2 Align the chart

Drag the four markers to the centers of the four corner patches of the chart (in this order: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left). Inner patches are placed by bilinear interpolation, so the chart can be at any angle or perspective.

3 Patches

Sampled vs. reference Rec.709 values. Click a reference swatch to override its value if your chart's printed values differ.

4 Fit & export

Download .cube
